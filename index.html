04 / METHOD & LIMITSSOURCES ARE PART OF THE INTERFACE

Wonder needs
ground truth.

The 3D scenes use NASA LOLA elevation models for Sites 01, 04 and 07; the preview shows Site04. Sun and Earth positions come from JPL Horizons for the selected point and UTC hour. The screen computes a local terrain horizon within 8 km.

The 5 m source grid contains approximately 90% interpolated pixels. The wide 3D mesh samples it at 41.7 m; an optional 2 km detail uses the source grid. Surface tone and cinematic fill light help reveal shape. The hero film is generated imagery, not a map of this site.
