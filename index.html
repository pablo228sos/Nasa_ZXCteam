04 / METHOD & LIMITSSOURCES ARE PART OF THE INTERFACE

Wonder needs
ground truth.

The global Moon uses NASA LRO visual maps for geographic context. The detailed 3D scenes use separate NASA LOLA elevation models for Sites 01, 04 and 07. The reference points use bundled JPL Horizons Sun and Earth positions; nearby chosen points resample the local 5 m LOLA grid.

The global globe is a visual atlas, not a 5 m simulator. Reference-site horizons reach 8 km; a moved point uses 6 km inside the local source tile. Roughly 90% of its 5 m pixels are interpolated. Sunlight direction in the atlas follows bundled JPL samples; the visible Sun symbol and distances are not to scale.
