03 / METHOD & LIMITSSOURCES ARE PART OF THE INTERFACE

Wonder needs
ground truth.

The 3D shape is built from NASA's LOLA Site04 elevation model. Sun and Earth positions come from JPL Horizons for the selected point and UTC hour. The screen computes a local terrain horizon within 8 km.

The 5 m source grid contains approximately 90% interpolated pixels; the live display samples it at 41.7 m. Surface tone and cinematic fill light help reveal shape. The hero film is generated imagery, not a map of this site.
